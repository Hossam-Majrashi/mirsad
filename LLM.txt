## Requirements

- Use `code` as the language code for locale resolution.
- Display each language using the name shown in `nativeLanguageName`.
- Arabic is the first language and English is the second language.
- Country-specific language variants (e.g. English (Australia), Spanish (Mexico), Portuguese (Brazil)) are intentionally excluded — only the base/generic language is kept.
- Script/writing-system variants are still included where they represent a distinct script, not a country (e.g. Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Bosnian (Cyrillic)).
- Do not add Hebrew (`he` or `iw`) or Arabic (Saudi Arabia) (`ar-SA`) — these are intentionally excluded.
- Ensure no duplicate language codes.
- Ensure all listed language codes are preserved exactly.
- RTL is enabled for Arabic (`ar`), Persian (`fa`), and Urdu (`ur`).

- Removed repeated regional dialects of an already-covered language: Batak Karo (btx), Batak Simalungun (bts), Batak Toba (bbc), Cantonese (yue).

- Script-writing-system variants of the same language are still kept (not dialects): Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Malay (Jawi), Punjabi (Shahmukhi), Kurdish (Kurmanji/Sorani).
